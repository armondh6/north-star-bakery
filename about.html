About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood-focused bakery built around the joy of sharing fresh food. Our goal is to make each visit welcoming, whether you are picking up breakfast or planning a celebration.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We value quality ingredients and thoughtful baking. We aim to work with trusted suppliers and choose ingredients that help our breads and treats taste their best.

Meet the Team

Our bakers prepare breads and pastries, while our front-counter team helps customers choose treats and discuss preorders. Every role contributes to a warm neighborhood experience.
